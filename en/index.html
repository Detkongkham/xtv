Experience and expertise you can rely on

Xupthavykhoun Sole Co., Ltd was registered as a legal company under the laws of Lao PDR on 1 July 2016. We supply and distribute medicines, medical products, laboratory equipment and all kinds of quality diagnostic reagents, and operate a security division that is modern and meets international standards.

The staff of each department bring more than 20 years of expertise in advising customers, and a professional after-sales department stands ready to serve you promptly.
