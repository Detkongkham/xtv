About this division

The medical equipment division supplies imaging systems, hospital equipment, diagnostic devices and surgical instruments. Every item is delivered with installation, training and after-sales service.

  • Products are selected for being modern and compliant with international standards, and are supplied with manufacturer documentation.
  • Equipment is installed and tested by the company's engineers, and users are trained before handover.
  • Reagents, consumables, spare parts and maintenance are provided throughout the period of use.
